Jacob English

Born and raised in St. George, Utah, and now based in Salt Lake City, I am a multidisciplinary marketer and creative with more than 4 years of marketing, startup, and project management experience. I graduated from Brigham Young University with a B.S. in Entrepreneurial Management. I’ve launched a beverage brand from scratch, amassing nearly 2 million views on social media, and grown a self-published international photography community of over 13,000 followers.

I thrive in environments where ideas and projects can be taken from start to finish. I work hard to be meticulous, reliable, and willing to go the extra mile to ensure a project's success. I’m always looking to learn and explore new ideas and points of view.

NOxyDERM

Marketing, E-commerce, and Project Management

NOxyDERM is an OTC wound care product. When I joined the team, the company had zero DTC sales that were not friend-of-a-friend referrals. I was tasked with building a new storefront, increasing organic and paid visibility, and launching Amazon as a channel. All of this had to be done while maintaining strict, compliant messaging according to FDA standards and navigating tighter claims reviews and lengthy feedback-and-review cycles.

I led a team of three running Google and Meta ads, managing paid and organic social plus SEO, and building the Amazon storefront. Together, we increased revenue by 6x and organic traffic by 45x over a year and a half.

Peer

Entrepreneurship, Project Management, and Marketing

I co-founded Peer (formerly Keda Kava) in college. Peer is a third space in a can, made of natural ingredients to promote relaxation and connection. We spent a year R&D'ing flavors and recipes in my kitchen, hosting tasting parties, and canning the drinks with a grant-funded canner. Before our rebrand to Peer, we sold 20,000 cans. I handled Amazon and Shopify site management and design, PPC and paid marketing efforts, and produced social content that led to nearly 2 million organic views across Instagram and TikTok.

In our rebrand, I project manage our team of four using ClickUp, Slack, and calendars to keep us aligned. Alongside directing and managing our marketing efforts, I partner with the team on creative direction through moodboarding and briefing, and personally designed more than 50 can and logo concepts throughout the process.

LQFS

Social Media Management, Publishing, and Graphic Design

Low Quality Film Society is an international film photography audience dedicated to sharing and promoting the work of film photographers around the world. We create guides and resources to help people share their work and improve their craft, and we've curated, designed, and printed over 15 zines, magazines, and books shipped across the globe.

Running this project sharpened my skills across the Adobe Suite, including Illustrator, Lightroom, Photoshop, and InDesign. I also manage the account day to day, handling posting, engagement, and audience growth. Through these efforts the page has grown to over 13,000 followers. By self-publishing, I learned how to take an idea from concept to finished printed product.
